# Community Code of Conduct

PhotoPop contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Assume good intent while remaining specific about evidence and impact.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish another person’s private information.
- Maintainers may remove content or participation that undermines a safe, productive project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Enforcement decisions should protect affected people and minimize unnecessary disclosure.
